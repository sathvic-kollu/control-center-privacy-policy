Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Control Center application (the "App") developed and published by SakhiApps. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Accessibility Service API

This App utilizes the Android Accessibility Service API strictly for core functionality. Specifically, the Accessibility Service is used to display the Control Center overlay over other apps and to capture system navigation gestures (such as returning to the Home screen, locking the screen, or showing Recent Apps) when triggered by the user via the custom control panel.

Important: We do NOT use the Accessibility Service API to collect, store, transmit, or share any personal, sensitive, or usage data. It is exclusively used for executing local, user-initiated actions.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The App operates entirely locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information (PII).

  • Screen Recording (Media Projection): The App requests Media Projection permissions to allow you to record your screen and capture internal audio. All screen recordings and screenshots are processed and saved locally on your device. We do not access, transmit, or upload these files to our servers.
  • Camera / Flashlight: The App requires Camera permission solely to toggle the device's flashlight on and off. We do not capture photos or videos.
  • System Settings: The App requires permission to modify system settings (such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Screen Brightness, and Volume) to function as a quick-settings panel. These changes are applied locally.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services, such as Google AdMob, to display advertisements within the App. These services may collect anonymous usage data or device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized ads. Please review the Google Privacy Policy for more details on how they handle data.
